01 About Babel Stud

A sanctuary for the Arabian horse

Babel Stud is an elite sanctuary in Jordan dedicated to the artistry, purity and prestige of the Arabian halter horse. Rooted in tradition and driven by perfection, Babel Stud combines distinguished bloodlines with world-class conditioning to breed, train and present Arabian beauty champions.

Vision

Setting the standard

To become the world's premier destination for Arabian horse breeding and halter training, setting the ultimate standard for beauty, elegance and world-champion titles.

Mission

Creating champions

To cultivate world-class Arabian halter champions through meticulous breeding selection, elite training and unrivaled care.
